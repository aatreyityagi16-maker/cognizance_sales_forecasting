# Business Insights Report

## Objective
Analyze historical monthly sales and estimate future sales.

## Dataset
36 months of sample monthly sales data from January 2023 through December 2025.

## Method
A Linear Regression model uses month number as the input and sales as the target.

## Insights
- Sales show an overall upward trend with monthly variation.
- The forecast provides a simple baseline for preliminary planning.
- Forecast information can support inventory, staffing and sales planning.
- A real business model should also consider promotions, holidays, pricing and external factors.

## Limitations
This is an educational project using a sample dataset. The model is a basic baseline and should be compared with stronger time-series methods before production use.

## Conclusion
The project demonstrates data loading, preprocessing, trend analysis, forecasting and visualization in one workflow.
